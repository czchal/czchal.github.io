Biography

I am a graduate research student pursuing a Master's degree in Aerospace Engineering at KAIST. As a member of the Laboratory for Information and Control Systems (LICS), I have the privilege of being advised by Professor Hanlim Choi. My work focuses on applying principles from modern control theory and mathematical optimization to solve complex challenges in spacecraft Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC). My specific focus is on developing robust and efficient algorithms that enable greater autonomy in spacecraft missions and mobile robotics.
